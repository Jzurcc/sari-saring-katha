<h1>Sari-Saring Katha v1.0 Full Release — Back Behind the Counter After 7 Months!</h1>

<p>Hello everyone!</p>

<p>After 7 months away, I finally got the time to sit back down, pour everything I wanted into <strong>Sari-Saring Katha</strong>, and bring it all the way to <strong>v1.0 Full Release</strong>.</p>

<p>When I last updated the game back in April (<code>v0.9.6-beta</code>), it was still a small, linear prototype: one customer at a time, a strict daily quota that kept climbing, and isolated early stories. Coming back to it, I wanted Karimlan to actually feel like a living Filipino <em>kanto</em> — a place with regulars who know each other, a counter you slowly make your own, and nights where you decide how hard to push your luck.</p>

<p>Here is everything new waiting for you in <strong>v1.0</strong>.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Major New Features</h2>

<h3>Reyna Mayari's Silver Ledger &amp; Moon Cards</h3>
<p>The old pass-or-fail daily quota is gone. Instead, you now work toward paying off a <strong>₱3,000 Silver Ledger</strong> at your own pace.</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Choose Your Nightly Tribute:</strong> Every evening at closing time, offer a <strong>Meager</strong>, <strong>Fair</strong>, or <strong>Lavish</strong> payment depending on how your day went.</li>
  <li><strong>8 Permanent Ledger Milestones:</strong> Every seal along the ₱3,000 ledger now grants both nightly Moon Card power and permanent daytime <em>tindahan</em> perks — from wholesale discounts and evening tip bonuses to unlocking Reyna Mayari ("Maria") as a daytime regular.</li>
  <li><strong>62 Moon Cards (Favors &amp; Omens):</strong> End each night by drafting from Common, Uncommon, and Rare cards that reshape tomorrow's run. Favors give clean boosts, while Omens offer risky trade-offs (like gusts of wind blowing your fridge door open in exchange for bigger cold-drink payouts).</li>
</ul>

<h3>Papa Ulap's Counter Radio &amp; Daily Street Events</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Tune the Radio:</strong> Buy and upgrade a transistor radio for your counter to hear <strong>Papa Ulap</strong> broadcast weather reports, folklore tidbits, commercials, and neighborhood news.</li>
  <li><strong>33 Neighborhood Events:</strong> From <em>Barangay Fiesta</em>, <em>Payday Rush</em>, and <em>Balikbayan Box Day</em> to tough days like <em>Petsa de Peligro</em>, <em>Scheduled Brownout</em>, and <em>Flooded Access Road</em>, every morning brings shifting cravings and store conditions.</li>
  <li><strong>Visitor Sightings &amp; Living Street Cameos:</strong> Watch the street outside or tune the radio to spot which regulars are in town today. All 11 neighbors now stroll past in both directions, and friends from connected stories will even walk down the road together.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Full Store Upgrade Tree &amp; Sign Painting Studio</h3>
<p>Press <strong>[U]</strong> to open the new skill tree and build up your <em>tindahan</em> step by step toward <strong>Open the Door</strong>:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Store Tiers 1–10:</strong> Unlock new snacks, canned goods, sachets, frozen ulam, drinks, and candy jars delivered in tossed plastic bags from Uncle Mario's tricycle.</li>
  <li><strong>Fridge Upgrades &amp; Coldness Meter:</strong> Keep the refrigerator door shut! Letting it warm up cuts chilled drink and frozen payouts until it cools back down.</li>
  <li><strong>Mingming the Store Cat:</strong> Feed and upgrade your counter cat to serve <strong>up to 3 customers at once</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Tambayan Bench:</strong> Give neighbors a place to hang out after shopping for passive XP, smalltalk, and second-round snack cravings.</li>
  <li><strong>Sto. Niño Altar, Business Permits, Maneki-Neko, Trash &amp; Recycling, and Wholesale Pass</strong> (unlocking Uncle Mario's daily discounted <em>Sulit Deals</em>).</li>
  <li><strong>Storefront Drawing Studio:</strong> Paint your own <strong>Roof Sign</strong> and <strong>Promo Poster</strong>! Placing matching category stickers on your poster highlights a Featured Category for extra demand and bonus cash, and your custom artwork even shows up on the Main Menu store.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Fair Pricing, Tips &amp; <em>Tawaran</em> Haggling</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Simple <code>-5</code> to <code>+5</code> Price Steps:</strong> Hold <strong>Alt</strong> (even while holding an item!) or press <strong>[L]</strong> for the Price Ledger to adjust prices with the scroll wheel and see active bonuses at a glance.</li>
  <li><strong>Customer Tips:</strong> Fair and generous pricing can earn appreciation tips and build neighborhood trust faster.</li>
  <li><strong>Tawaran Card Duel:</strong> Push a markup a bit too far and a customer might challenge you to a timed verbal haggling card duel instead of walking straight out.</li>
</ul>

<h3>5 Filipino Street &amp; Folklore Minigames</h3>
<p>Break up counter shifts with five short minigames woven into daily life and character stories:</p>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Tawaran</strong> — Read customer moods and play the right banter cards to seal a marked-up sale.</li>
  <li><strong>Endless Sipa</strong> — Keep the washer airborne and chase neighborhood high scores.</li>
  <li><strong>Teks</strong> — Classic street card flips against Buboy and regulars.</li>
  <li><strong>Dusk Lamp Mosquito Swat</strong> — Swat mosquitoes diving and climbing from every angle around the storefront bulb after 4:00 PM while dodging fast golden fireflies.</li>
  <li><strong>Anting-Anting Tracing</strong> — Steady your hand to trace protective talisman sigils with Brahim.</li>
</ul>

<h3>11 Neighbors, Connected Stories, Folklore Journal &amp; Epilogues</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Meet Lolo Pilo:</strong> A retired 1968 <em>sipa</em> champion with a sharp eye and an old secret joins the cast, bringing the neighborhood roster to <strong>11 regulars</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Interwoven 6-Chapter Arcs:</strong> Neighbors no longer live in separate bubbles. Their stories cross paths across shared storylines, and your choices (forgiving <em>utang</em>, offering substitutes when you're out of stock, winning or losing minigames) shape how their lives turn out without locking you out of finishing their story.</li>
  <li><strong>Folklore Journal (<code>[J]</code>):</strong> Earn <strong>Reputation Ranks I–VI</strong> with each regular to unmask their true folk creature identity at Rank 2, unlock perks, Counter Notes, Chapter Prerequisite hints, and illustrated entries on real Philippine folklore creatures (with credited community/archival artwork).</li>
  <li><strong>Karimlan Epilogues:</strong> Sealing the Silver Ledger unlocks a multi-card ending chronicle showing what became of every neighbor based on how you treated them.</li>
</ul>
