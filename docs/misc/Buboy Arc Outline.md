# Buboy — Story Arc Outline

### 3 Arcs · 9 Interactions · Playfulness → Observation → Understanding

---

## Character Foundation

**Buboy** is a child *Bungisngis* — in Philippine folklore, a one-eyed giant who is constantly laughing or giggling. As a young Bungisngis, Buboy is just a sweet, perpetually giggling one-eyed boy. His mother frequently interrupts his playtime to send him on errands to your sari-sari store. He looks up immensely to his neighbor, Kuya Kap. Because he’s a child and often distracted, he almost never remembers the actual names of the items his mother asks him to buy, relying on the player to solve his "riddles" based on his innocent descriptions.

### Dialogue Filter (Global)

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Core Want** | To finish his errands quickly so he can go back to playing with his friends, and to make people smile. |
| **Strategy** | Making a game out of his forgetfulness. If he can't remember the item, he turns it into a riddle, giggling to diffuse any frustration. |
| **What He Hides** | Absolutely nothing. He is pure innocence, which makes his blunt observations (especially regarding Kuya Kap) incredibly piercing to adults. |
| **Voice** | High energy. Peppers dialogue with giggles ("Hihi!", "Hehe!"). Uses polite Filipino honorifics ("Kuya," "Ate," "Po," "Opo") but speaks in modern, simple English. Very literal in how he views the world. |

### The Arc at a Glance

```
ARC 1 — THE ERRAND BOY  →  ARC 2 — THE CURIOUS MIND    →  ARC 3 — THE GIGGLING GIANT
He introduces the game.    His innocence hits hard.       He witnesses the change.
Player learns to guess.    Player tracks Kap via Buboy.   Player gets the full picture.
```

**Collectible Reward:** *Buboy's Masterpiece* — A crayon drawing of a smiling one-eyed kid, a towering gentle giant, and the sari-sari store. It’s slightly crumpled from being in his pocket.

## ARC 1 — "THE ERRAND BOY"

**Thematic Purpose:** Introduce the functional Riddle Mechanic natively into the dialogue. Establish his playfulness, his forgetfulness, and his constant giggling. Lay the groundwork for his adoration of Kuya Kap.

**Arc-End State:** The player knows how to handle Buboy's riddle-based requests. They know he is neighbors with Kuya Kap.

---

### Chapter 1 — *"The Memory Game"*

**Scene Beat:** Buboy rushes in, still out of breath from playing outside. He immediately establishes his gimmick: he's forgotten what he's here for.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Buboy is a kid sent on an errand.
- He communicates his needs via riddles/descriptions.
- He giggles constantly.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He wants to get the item without getting in trouble with his mom for forgetting what it was called.

**What Else Is Happening:**
He’s bouncing on his toes, looking back over his shoulder at his friends playing in the street.

**Subtext Through Commerce:**
The player must decode the description. (e.g., Sardines).

**Key Beat:**
> *"Hi! Mama told me to run here quick. Hihi! But... I kind of forgot what she told me to buy."*
> *(He taps his chin, giggling).*
> *"Umm... it's a silver can, and there's a big fish with a hook on it! [HighestCannedGood]! Do you know what it is?"*

**Information Out:** The riddle mechanic is established effortlessly.

---

### Chapter 2 — *"The Big Neighbor"*

**Scene Beat:** Buboy comes in to buy an ingredient. He mentions Kuya Kap for the first time, establishing their dynamic.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Buboy lives near Kuya Kap.
- Kuya Kap is a "safe adult" for the neighborhood kids.
- Another riddle to solve.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To share how fun his day is going. To him, errands are just an extension of playtime.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Kuya Kap was watching us play earlier! He's so tall, he can get our ball when it gets stuck on the roof. Hihi!"*
> *(He suddenly gasps, remembering his errand).*
> *"Oh no, Mama will get mad! I need... um... the red bag with the duck on it! It tastes salty and crunchy! [HighestSnack]! Do you know?"*

**Information Out:** Kuya Kap is established as a gentle giant in the eyes of the children.

---

### Chapter 3 — *"The Smell of Smoke"*

**Scene Beat:** Buboy is here for soap. His innocent observations start brushing up against Kuya Kap's hidden struggles.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Kuya Kap's smoking is highly noticeable to a child.
- Buboy doesn't judge it; he just states factually what he experiences.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He’s dirty from playing and his mom sent him to get cleaning supplies. He's just making conversational connections in his head.

**Key Beat:**
> *(He wipes dirt off his cheek).*
> *"I need a strong bath today. Mama said I smell like outside. Kuya Kap always smells like weird smoke... I wonder how he takes a bath? Hihi!"*
> *"Anyway, Mama said I smell like outside, so I need to take a bath. I need the soap! The blue rectangle thing... [HighestDetergent]! What's it called again?"*

**Information Out:** The adult reality of Kap's addiction begins bleeding into Buboy's innocent worldview.

---

## ARC 2 — "THE CURIOUS MIND"

**Thematic Purpose:** Buboy functions as the unintentional catalyst for Kuya Kap's arc. Because Buboy doesn't have an adult filter, he asks the questions adults are too polite to ask.

**Arc-End State:** The player sees exactly why Kuya Kap felt so heavily burdened by Buboy's questions in Kap's own arc.

---

### Chapter 4 — *"The Direct Question"*

**Scene Beat:** This parallels Kuya Kap's "For Buboy" interaction. Buboy recounts his conversation with Kap directly to the player.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Buboy asked Kuya Kap to quit smoking.
- He doesn't understand why stopping a bad habit is complicated.
- A riddle for a snack he wants for himself.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He’s slightly confused by adult behavior. He wants the player to validate his childlike logic.

**What Else Is Happening:**
He isn't bouncing as much today. He's genuinely pondering.

**Key Beat:**
> *"I told Kuya Kap on Sunday, 'If that smoke makes you cough, why don't you just throw the box away?'"*
> *(He tilts his head, confused).*
> *"He didn't answer me. He just patted my head. Adults are so weird sometimes. Hihi!"*
> *"Opo, can I have... the green packet with the nuts and peas? [HighestSnack]! I have a few pesos left over!"*

**Information Out:** The player hears the exact moment that broke Kuya Kap's defense, straight from the source.

---

### Chapter 5 — *"Where is He?"*

**Scene Beat:** This parallels Kuya Kap's missing days. Buboy notices Kap's absence from the neighborhood watch.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Kuya Kap is isolating himself.
- Buboy misses him but doesn't understand the gravity of it.
- Another household riddle.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He wants things to go back to normal so his neighbor can get their balls off the roof again.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Kuya Kap hasn't been sitting on his chair outside lately. The street is so quiet! Hihi, maybe he's playing hide and seek?"*
> *(He fishes into his pocket for coins).*
> *"Mama needs something for the kitchen. A red circle of meat! [HighestMeal]! Do you know?"*

**Information Out:** Showing the ripple effect of Kap's isolation on the community.

---

### Chapter 6 — *"The Idea"*

**Scene Beat:** Buboy casually reveals that he's the one who planted the idea about the White Stone in Kap's head.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Buboy gave Kap the advice about the stone.
- Pure, unadulterated innocent wisdom.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He feels proud of himself for giving an adult advice.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Hihi! I saw Kuya Kap holding his shiny white rock today. He looked sad looking at it. So I told him, 'Mama said if you don't need something anymore, you should give it to a nice person!'"*
> *"Like you! I think you're nice! Hihi!"*
> *"Oh no, Mama will scold me. Fast, I need the... the dark drink inside a glass bottle that makes you burp! [HighestDrink]!"*

**Information Out:** Buboy is the driving force behind Kap's gift to the player.

---

## ARC 3 — "THE GIGGLING GIANT"

**Thematic Purpose:** The resolution. Buboy sees the change in Kuya Kap. He continues his riddles but with an added layer of joy, validating Kap's hard work.

**Arc-End State:** The player sees Kuya Kap's triumph through the kid's eyes. Buboy brings a reward for the player's patience.

---

### Chapter 7 — *"The Secret"*

**Scene Beat:** Buboy is exceptionally giddy today. He has a secret about Kuya Kap.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Buboy witnessed Kuya Kap buying candy/gum.
- The shift is complete.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He loves knowing a secret and wants to share it via a riddle.

**Key Beat:**
> *(He is practically vibrating with giggles).*
> *"Guess what! Guess what! Kuya Kap doesn't have his white box anymore! He gave me his shiny stone, but I told him I'm too small for it. Hihi!"*
> *"Today, he asked me what my favorite sweet is. Can you guess? It's small, it's cheap... [HighestCandy]!"*

**Information Out:** Buboy is thrilled. Kap made a healthy choice that brings him closer to the kid.

---

### Chapter 8 — *"No More Smoke"*

**Scene Beat:** Buboy brings a complicated household riddle from his mom, but he is completely unbothered because Kuya Kap played with him.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Kuya Kap is fully integrated back into the neighborhood, healthier.
- Buboy's world is bright again.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To run his errand easily, feeling perfectly safe and happy in his town.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Kuya Kap helped us fix our kite today! And he didn't smell like weird smoke... he smelled like strawberry! Just like a giant kid! Hihi!"*
> *"Umm... Mama wrote this one down but I lost the paper. It's a loud bag of crunchy pig skin! [HighestSnack]! Please tell me you know it, hihi!"*

**Information Out:** The emotional capstone to Kuya Kap and Buboy's intertwining arcs.

---

### Chapter 9 — *"The Masterpiece"*

**Scene Beat:** Buboy comes in for himself today. No riddle. He knows exactly what he wants. He brings the player a gift.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Buboy remembers a name perfectly when he cares about it.
- He views the player and Kuya Kap as important figures in his life.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To give a gift. Just pure, giggling gratitude.

**What Else Is Happening:**
He proudly slaps his drawing onto the counter.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Ate/Kuya! I don't have a guessing game today. I know exactly what I want. One [HighestMeal], please!"*
> *(He hands over the exact coins, and pushes a piece of paper across the counter).*
> *"I made this in school! That's me, and that huge one is Kuya Kap! And that box is your store! You helped him smell like strawberries, right? Hihi! Bye!"*
> *(He runs off laughing before you can say anything).*

**Collectible Unlocked:** 🖍️ *Buboy's Masterpiece* — *"A crayon drawing of a giggling one-eyed kid, a giant man, and the sari-sari store. It’s slightly crumpled, but drawn with obvious love."*
