# Rodel — Story Arc Outline

### 3 Arcs · 9 Interactions · The Stranger → The Searcher → The Wanderer

---

## Character Foundation

**Rodel** is a *Litao*, an ancient Philippine water spirit. While he walks on two legs and appears mostly human, he is entirely out of his element on dry land. The air is too thin, the food is too dry, and human customs baffle him. He is the husband of **Nerissa, the Kataw** (Queen of the Mermaids), but they were separated during a catastrophic shift in the river currents. He has wandered into Karimlan searching for her. He is unfailingly polite, carries himself with the quiet dignity of a prince, but treats everyday land routines as if he is an alien on a strange planet.

### Dialogue Filter (Global)

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Core Want** | To find his missing wife, Nerissa, and to survive long enough on dry land to do it. |
| **Strategy** | Extreme politeness masking deep confusion. He treats the player as an "Ambassador of the Dry Realm." |
| **What He Hides** | The creeping despair that he might dry out completely before he finds her. |
| **Voice** | Formal, poetic, but highly aquatic. He uses ocean and river metaphors for everything. Example: "The current of this road is very slow," rather than "Traffic is bad." |

### The Scene Architecture (Masterclass Applied)

* **The Timer:** Rodel is constantly fighting dehydration. He frequently checks his skin, which begins to crack or flake the longer he stands in the store. He has to cut conversations short to go submerge himself in a nearby creek.
* **The Collision:** He interacts with the store's terrestrial items with bewilderment. Land currency (paper money melting in his wet hands), canned fish, and dust are constant points of friction.
* **Subtext Through Commerce:** Rodel overwhelmingly buys `[HighestDrink]`. When he doesn't, he buys items attempting to mimic the sea (e.g., salty snacks), only to be horribly confused by artificial flavors.

### The Arc at a Glance

```
ARC 1 — THE OUTSIDER         →  ARC 2 — THE SEARCH           →  ARC 3 — THE NEXT RIVER
He is baffled by land rules.    He reveals his lost love.      He realizes she isn't here.
Player explains the town.       Player learns of Nerissa.      He says a final goodbye.
```

**Collectible Reward:** *Tears of the Kataw* — A perfectly smooth, iridescent blue pearl wrapped in a piece of dried sea kelp.

## ARC 1 — "THE OUTSIDER"

**Thematic Purpose:** Establish his "fish out of water" nature (literally). Introduce the comedic yet sad friction of an aquatic spirit trying to understand human commerce.

**Arc-End State:** The player understands he is not human, and that he is looking for someone.

---

### Chapter 1 — *"The Dry Air"*

**Scene Beat:** Rodel is standing at the counter. He is dripping wet, leaving a puddle on the floor. He is frantically seeking hydration.

**Information to Communicate:**
* He is an aquatic being.
* He is unfamiliar with land customs.
* He is heavily reliant on water.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
Water to survive the thin, dry air, but he is trying to be a polite tourist.

**Timer:** His breathing is shallow. He needs moisture immediately.

**Key Beat:**
> *(A small puddle of water has already formed at his feet. He looks intensely uncomfortable).*
> *"Greetings, keeper of the dry shelves. The... the atmosphere here is incredibly thin. Like breathing sand."*
> *"I require your finest `[HighestDrink]`. I am unaccustomed to how you land-walkers endure without the deep pressure."*
> *(He pays with coins that feel remarkably cold and smell like kelp. He immediately pours half the drink over his head, sighing in relief).*

---

### Chapter 2 — *"The False Salt"*

**Scene Beat:** Rodel tries to experience human "ocean" food to cope with homesickness.

**Information to Communicate:**
* He is deeply homesick.
* Human imitations of the ocean confuse him.
* He drops the first hint of his wife.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To taste something familiar, only to be disappointed by artificial preservatives.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Tell me, do you carry anything that tastes of the waking tide? The wind here smells only of exhaust fumes and hot soil."*
> *(He settles for a `[HighestSnack]` that is supposedly "sea salt" flavored).*
> *"Fascinating. Your people crush the salt, yet remove the life from it. It crunches like a hermit crab shell, but it tastes entirely hollow. Nerissa would find this absolutely tragic."*
> *"Keep the change. I must find a humid place to rest."*

---

### Chapter 3 — *"The Metal Trap"*

**Scene Beat:** Rodel notices canned goods and has a deeply aquatic, horrified reaction.

**Information to Communicate:**
* His wife is a Kataw (Queen of Fish).
* His worldview is utterly alien to human commerce.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To understand why humans are so brutal to the subjects of his wife.

**What Else Is Happening:**
He stares at the shelves with wide, unblinking eyes.

**Key Beat:**
> *"A question, if I may. Those metal cylinders on your shelf... you trap fish inside them? In acidic tomato blood? Forever?"*
> *(He shudders, paying for a `[HighestDrink]`).*
> *"My wife is the Queen of the Shoals. If she saw this, she would summon a typhoon to swallow this building. I shall pray she never wanders into this aisle. It is barbaric. Truly."*

---

## ARC 2 — "THE SEARCH"

**Thematic Purpose:** Shift from comedic confusion to romantic tragedy. Rodel reveals why he is forcing himself to suffer the dry land.

**Arc-End State:** The player learns about Nerissa, and they help him realize she is not in this town.

---

### Chapter 4 — *"The Severed Current"*

**Scene Beat:** Rodel's skin is visibly cracking. He is exhausted. He openly asks about his missing wife.

**Information to Communicate:**
* His wife went missing in a storm.
* He is searching the towns one by one.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
Information. He drops the polite tourist facade in favor of desperate inquiry.

**Timer:** The cracking of his skin is visually alarming; he cannot stay long.

**Key Beat:**
> *"I am looking for a woman. She smells of petrichor and deep water. She has... she has eyes like crushed pearls."*
> *(He buys a `[HighestDrink]` just to press the cold bottle against his neck).*
> *"We were separated during the great monsoon. The river currents shifted abruptly, and the undertow pulled her toward the surface world. Have you seen her? Nerissa. Please tell me the land has not kept her."*

---

### Chapter 5 — *"The Broken Compass"*

**Scene Beat:** He attempts to use aquatic magic on land, but human infrastructure blocks it.

**Information to Communicate:**
* He is lost.
* The concrete of the town is hindering his search.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
Directions, using terms humans don't use.

**Key Beat:**
> *(He holds a massive, iridescent seashell to his ear, looking frustrated).*
> *"The concrete rivers... your 'roads'... they confuse the resonance. The shell only echoes with the sound of car engines. I cannot hear her song."*
> *(He buys a `[HighestCandy]` to soothe his dry throat).*
> *"Which way does the great water flow from here? If I follow the asphalt, will it lead to an ocean, or just more stone?"*

---

### Chapter 6 — *"The Bitter Truth"*

**Scene Beat:** Rodel accepts the reality. He spoke to the local spirits (perhaps Danilo or Kap) and they confirmed no Kataw has been through here.

**Information to Communicate:**
* Nerissa isn't in Karimlan.
* He must continue his agonizing journey.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To process his grief, using the store as a quiet anchor before he leaves.

**Key Beat:**
> *"I spoke to the one with many eyes down the street. He sees all that passes through here. He has not seen her."*
> *(He slumps, paying for his usual `[HighestDrink]`).*
> *"She is not in Karimlan. The wind here carries no trace of her scales. I have walked upon this burning stone for nothing."*
> *"I must prepare to depart. The next river is many miles away."*

---

## ARC 3 — "THE WANDERER"

**Thematic Purpose:** Closure. Rodel has learned to survive marginally better on land, and he prepares for a permanent departure. He says a touching goodbye to the player.

**Arc-End State:** Rodel leaves the game permanently, leaving behind his collectible.

---

### Chapter 7 — *"Land Rations"*

**Scene Beat:** Rodel is preparing for his overland journey. He buys actual food, showing he has adapted slightly.

**Information to Communicate:**
* He is gearing up for a tough journey.
* He has gained a begrudging respect for human survival.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To prepare efficiently, using what he's learned about the dry realm.

**Key Beat:**
> *"I am plotting a course to the next province. There is a deep lake there. She might be hiding in its depths."*
> *(He pays for a `[HighestMeal]`).*
> *"I will need this. Your human food is unpleasantly dry, but it provides excellent energy for walking. I suppose I must commend your species' endurance. The dry world is a brutal place to survive."*

---

### Chapter 8 — *"The Ambassador"*

**Scene Beat:** Rodel reflects on his time in Karimlan. He thanks the player.

**Information to Communicate:**
* The player made his stay bearable.
* He views the player as a true friend.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To formally express his gratitude before he formally leaves.

**Key Beat:**
> *"When I first breached the surface, I believed all land-walkers to be as loud and chaotic as your metal carriages."*
> *(He buys a `[HighestDrink]` one last time).*
> *"But you... you possess the stillness of a deep trench. You allowed a stranger from the water to rest at your shores without asking for his scales. You are a fine ambassador for the dry realm."*

---

### Chapter 9 — *"The Final Departure"*

**Scene Beat:** Epilogue. Rodel is leaving for good. He doesn't buy anything. He has no reason to pretend to be a customer anymore.

**Information to Communicate:**
* He is leaving permanently.
* He gives the player his reward as a token of the deep.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To give a parting gift and walk away toward the horizon.

**What Else Is Happening:**
The audio shifts. Rain begins to fall lightly outside the store, completely ignoring the game's actual weather state, signaling his aquatic magic returning.

**Key Beat:**
> *"The tide is turning. I must ride the rains out of this valley."*
> *(He reaches into his pocket and places a small, glowing pearl on the counter. It is wrapped in what looks like fresh, green kelp).*
> *"I cannot buy anything today. My pockets are empty of your metal coins. But I leave you this. It was meant for Nerissa... but I will find her another. Keep it close. It reminds the water that you are a friend."*
> *(He turns from the counter. He tilts his head up into the rain, smiles, and walks away into the mist).*

**Collectible Unlocked:** 🦪 *Tears of the Kataw* — *"A perfectly round, iridescent blue pearl. If you hold it to your ear, you don't hear the ocean—you hear a woman singing a very sad, very faint song."*
