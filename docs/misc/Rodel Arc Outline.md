# Rodel's Narrative Arc Outline: The Ambassador of the Abyssal Courts

**Character**: Rodel (Litao Water Spirit)
**Persona**: "Alien Ambassador"—Archaic, extremely formal, treats the store as a diplomatic outpost in a hostile, dry territory.
**Quest**: Searching for his wife, Nerissa (a Kataw), who was swept away by a rogue current tainted by human waste.

---

## Arc 1: The High Pressure of Humidity (Stranger)

### Chapter 1: The Atmosphere of Sand
*   **Request**: `HighestDrink`
*   **Dialogue**: Rodel enters, complaining about the "abrasive" terrestrial atmosphere. Compares air to ground glass. He needs "hydro-stasis stabilization."
*   **Standard**: Smart dialogue cues his physical discomfort and the sound of his heavy, wet footsteps slowing.

### Chapter 2: The Choice of Salt (Branching Choice)
*   **Request**: `Snacks`
*   **Choice**: Sea-Salt Chips vs. Salted Nuts.
*   **Outcome**: Rodel reacts uniquely to the texture of his chosen "land-crunch" as a mimicry of the sea's depth. He notes the strange alchemy of soil and salt.
*   **Standard**: No parentheses. Rodel interprets the transaction as a diplomatic exchange.

### Chapter 3: The Metal Coffins
*   **Request**: `HighestDrink`
*   **Dialogue**: Rodel is horrified by the sight of canned sardines. He calls them "Silver-Swimmers in iron coffins" and notes his wife's protective nature over her subjects.
*   **Standard**: Use timed pauses [pause=0.3] to show his genuine shock and mourning for the "trapped" fish.

## Arc 2: The Drying Heart (Searcher)

### Chapter 4: The Cracking Shield
*   **Request**: `HighestDrink`
*   **Dialogue**: Rodel admits his gills are becoming "brittle." He explains Nerissa's curiosity about "the light from above" and how it led to her disappearance.
*   **Standard**: Remove thought parentheses; Rodel’s vulnerability is shown through his slowing speech rhythm.

### Chapter 5: The Forked Tide (Branching Choice)
*   **Request**: `Snacks`
*   **Choice**: Search the **Coastal Cliffs** vs. the **Mangrove Delta**.
*   **Outcome**: Rodel discusses the strategic value of height vs. the humid comfort of roots. He prepares for a long trek.
*   **Standard**: The player's advice is treated as an "Intelligence Report."

### Chapter 6: Echo of Emptiness
*   **Request**: `HighestDrink`
*   **Dialogue**: Rodel returns, exhausted. He describes how the land "devours" memories of the sea. His search was fruitless.
*   **Standard**: Use dialogue to show his physical exhaustion—his voice becomes "thirstier."

## Arc 3: The Evaporating Spirit (Wanderer)

### Chapter 7: Terrestrial Rations
*   **Request**: `HighestMeal`
*   **Dialogue**: Before his final trek to the inland lake, Rodel praises the "grit" of land-life. He notes that humans are "masters of suffering" for living in the "fire" of the sun.
*   **Standard**: Rodel shows respect for the Storekeeper without breaking his formal ambassador persona.

### Chapter 8: Ambassador's Choice (Final Branching Choice)
*   **Request**: `HighestDrink`
*   **Choice**: **Return to the Deep** (to save himself) vs. **Eternal Watch** (stay on land until the end).
*   **Outcome**: Rodel reflects on how the "surface fluid" has changed his veins. He provides a final reaction based on the player's immediate advice.
*   **Standard**: The weight of the choice is felt through Rodel's long pauses and formal acknowledgement of the player's wisdom.

### Chapter 9: Rains of Farewell
*   **Request**: VISIT (Farewell)
*   **Dialogue**: It is raining. Rodel believes the sky is "weeping" for his departure. He gives the player the **Tear of the Kataw** as a final diplomatic gift. He notes that the Storekeeper is a "friend to the water."
*   **Standard**: Use the arrival of the rain (via dialogue) as the backdrop for his exit.
*   **Reward**: Unlocks the `Tears of the Kataw` collectible.
