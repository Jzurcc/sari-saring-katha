# Brahim — Story Arc Outline

### 3 Arcs · 9 Interactions · The Modern Shaman

---

## Character Foundation

**Brahim** is the town's resident *Albularyo* (Filipino folk healer/shaman), but he is decidedly modern. He inherited the gift (and the heavy responsibility) from his late grandfather, but he navigates the spirit world with a very Gen-Z, laid-back attitude. He occasionally drops modern slang ("Skibidi," "lowkey," "negative aura," "fr fr") naturally, but when he speaks about the spirits, he drops incredibly profound, ancient wisdom. His cozy storyline revolves around finding a balance between the heavy, ancient traditions of his ancestors and his desire to just be a normal, chill guy.

### Dialogue Filter (Global)

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Core Want** | To heal his community and appease the local spirits without burning out or losing his modern identity. |
| **Strategy** | Masks the intense, often terrifying reality of his job with extreme casually and Gen-Z slang. |
| **What He Hides** | Imposter syndrome. He worries his grandfather's spirit is disappointed that he relies on modern shortcuts instead of the old, traditional ways. |
| **Voice** | Effortlessly chill. Mixes deep, poetic folklore wisdom with completely out-of-place internet slang. Never sounds panicked, even when talking about demons. |

### The Scene Architecture (Masterclass Applied)

* **The Timer:** Brahim is always on the clock. His phone is constantly buzzing with texts from locals needing exorcisms, hex removals, or fever cures. He's always trying to wrap up the conversation to go save someone.
* **The Collision:** Brahim treats the Sari-Sari store as his pharmacy. He examines normal items deeply, looking for magical properties.
* **Subtext Through Commerce:** Brahim frequently buys mundane items (`[HighestSnack]`, `[HighestDrink]`) to use as substitutes for ancient herbs, proving his core conflict: he’s improvising modern magic.

### The Arc at a Glance

```

Introduces his modern magic.    He feels he is failing.        He merges old and new.
```

## ARC 1 — "THE IMPROVISER"

**Thematic Purpose:** Establish his unique juxtaposition of ancient magic and modern life. Introduce the mechanic of him using store goods for supernatural rituals.

**Arc-End State:** The player sees him as a highly capable, if slightly unorthodox, healer.

---

### Chapter 1 — *"The MSG Offering"*

**Scene Beat:** Brahim arrives, scrolling urgently on his phone. He needs supplies for a ritual but ran out of traditional ingredients.

**Information to Communicate:**
* He is an Albularyo.
* He uses modern slang.
* The spirits are adapting to the modern world.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
A quick substitute for an offering so he can get back to work.

**Timer:** His phone buzzes incessantly. "Tita Baby's house is definitely haunted right now."

**Key Beat:**
> *"Yo. Boss. Quick question, do you have any `[HighestSnack]`? I'm completely out of raw rock salt."*
> *(He inspects the packaging, nodding in approval).*
> *"I've got a Duwende acting up in Sector 4. Usually, you offer them unsalted rice to show respect, but honestly? The newer spirits are lowkey addicted to MSG now. They won't leave unless you give them artificial flavoring. It's wild."*
> *(His phone buzzes loudly).*
> *"Skibidi toilet, Tita Baby is texting in all caps again. Keep the change, boss!"*

---

### Chapter 2 — *"The Aura Check"*

**Scene Beat:** Brahim comes in looking satisfied. He successfully cured a local kid.

**Information to Communicate:**
* He is genuinely good at his job.
* Drops actual profound knowledge.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To grab a drink to rehydrate after an exorcism.

**Key Beat:**
> *"That kid who got sick yesterday? Na-nuno (cursed by an anthill). Tried to kick a dirt mound. Minus 1000 aura, honestly."*
> *(He pays for a `[HighestDrink]`, taking a long sip).*
> *"You really gotta respect the unseen, fr. The earth remembers every footstep. If you walk into someone else's house without saying 'Excuse me', you bring their anger home with you. It’s just basic boundaries, bro."*

---

### Chapter 3 — *"The Wrong Vibes"*

**Scene Beat:** Brahim arrives seeking an ingredient to banish a spirit, but he refuses to buy a specific item because the "color is wrong."

**Information to Communicate:**
* The rules of his magic are strict, even if he improvises.
* He buys his own food here too.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
Lunch, but he can't turn off his "healer vision."

**Key Beat:**
> *(He reaches for an item, then suddenly pulls his hand back).*
> *"Whoa. Red packaging. Nope. Big nope."*
> *"Red attracts the Kapre that lives behind the basketball court. Bro is a menace. I wore a red supreme shirt once and he threw a whole mango at my head. Just give me a `[HighestMeal]` in a blue or green wrapper, please. Gotta keep the vibes purely neutral today."*

---

## ARC 2 — "THE BURNOUT"

**Thematic Purpose:** The emotional dip. Brahim is taking on everyone else's negative energy, and his grandfather's legacy is weighing him down.

**Arc-End State:** Brahim realizes he needs to heal himself before he can heal others.

---

### Chapter 4 — *"The Heavy Shoulders"*

**Scene Beat:** Brahim looks exhausted. His posture is slumped. The typical Gen-Z energy is gone.

**Information to Communicate:**
* The job takes a physical and emotional toll.
* He is absorbing negative energy.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
Stimulation to stay awake.

**Timer:** He rubs his temples constantly, wincing at the store's lights.

**Key Beat:**
> *"I need a `[HighestDrink]`. The strongest one you have. Please."*
> *(He leans heavily against the counter).*
> *"I cleansed four houses today. When you pull bad energy out of someone, it doesn't just disappear. It sticks to your hands. You have to ground it. But I'm just... too tired to touch grass right now. I’m basically carrying a whole barangay's worth of bad vibes with me."*

---

### Chapter 5 — *"The Judgment of the Ancestors"*

**Scene Beat:** Brahim explicitly states his core conflict. He is hallucinating (or actually seeing) his grandfather judging him.

**Information to Communicate:**
* He feels like an imposter.
* His modern methods feel like a betrayal of tradition.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
Validation from the player, the one person who isn't a patient.

**Key Beat:**
> *(He stares blankly at a `[HighestSnack]` before paying for it).*
> *"I tried the old Latin chants last night. For a possession. It didn't work. I panicked, so I just played a frequency out of my Bluetooth speaker and told the spirit it was trespassing. It worked perfectly."*
> *(He looks down).*
> *"My Lolo used to visit me in my dreams to guide me. But lately... he just stands there, shaking his head. I'm doing it wrong. I'm faking it, bro. I’m just a guy with a Spotify premium account."*

---

### Chapter 6 — *"The Empty Battery"*

**Scene Beat:** Brahim refuses to look at his phone. It's ringing non-stop, but he ignores it.

**Information to Communicate:**
* He is setting a boundary.
* The climax of his internal arc.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To put himself first for once.

**Key Beat:**
> *(His phone rings. He mutes it and shoves it deep into his pocket).*
> *"They can wait. The ghosts can wait. The hexes can wait."*
> *"Give me a `[HighestMeal]`. Not for an offering. For me. If I don't stop and eat an actual meal, my soul is going to detach from my body mid-afternoon, and I am NOT dealing with astral projection today. That is way too much effort."*

---

## ARC 3 — "THE NEW BALANCE"

**Thematic Purpose:** Brahim reconciles the old with the new. He discovers that his grandfather isn't disappointed; tradition just looks different in every generation.

**Arc-End State:** Brahim is back to his chill self, operating at maximum effectiveness.

---

### Chapter 7 — *"Touching Grass"*

**Scene Beat:** Brahim looks glowing and refreshed. He found his balance.

**Information to Communicate:**
* He took a real break.
* He feels aligned again.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To share his renewed energy.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Yo. Look at me. Aura is absolutely restored. 100% capacity."*
> *(He pays for a `[HighestDrink]` with a bright smile).*
> *"I turned my phone off for three days. Went to the river. Actually touched grass. My Lolo always said water washes away the spiritual dirt, I just forgot to actually listen. Sometimes the ancient wisdom is literally just 'go outside and take a break, idiot.'"*

---

### Chapter 8 — *"The Hybrid Magic"*

**Scene Beat:** Brahim explains how he merged his lolo's teachings with his modern life.

**Information to Communicate:**
* His imposter syndrome is gone.
* His magic is stronger than ever.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To grab a quick snack before a *successful* ritual.

**Key Beat:**
> *"I stopped trying to sound like my Lolo. He was born in the 1930s, of course his magic sounded different."*
> *"I did a cleansing this morning. I used the old salt circles, but I burned sage while playing a lofi hip-hop playlist. The spirit didn't just leave, bro, it fell asleep. The ancestors aren't mad we changed things. They’re just waiting for us to make the magic our own."*
> *"Hand me a `[HighestSnack]`. Gotta restock my inventory."*

---

### Chapter 9 — *"The Digital Rune"*

**Scene Beat:** The epilogue. Brahim gives the player a gift to protect the store.

**Information to Communicate:**
* He considers the store part of his protected territory.
* He gives the collectible.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To express gratitude by ensuring the player's safety.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Listen, your store is like a sanctuary hub. A lot of heavy energy passes through these doors every day. I see who comes and goes."*
> *(He places a small, black USB flash drive on the counter. Intricate, glowing lines are carved into the plastic).*
> *"Give me my usual `[HighestDrink]`. And keep that flash drive. I carved some Baybayin protection runes into the casing. It’s got hours of ambient beats on it too. Plug it in... it keeps the bad vibes and the dark spirits completely out of your algorithm. Stay safe, boss."*
