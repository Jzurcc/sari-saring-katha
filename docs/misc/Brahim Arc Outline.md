# Brahim's Narrative Arc: The Skibidi Shaman

**Character**: Brahim (Modern Albularyo)
**Persona**: "The Modern Knowledge Giver"—A bridge between ancient spirits and digital culture. He's funny because he's dead serious; he sincerely treats internet slang (Amogus, Skibidi, Six Seven, Touch Grass) as sacred, newly-discovered Albularyo ritual terms. He doesn't think he's being cool; he thinks he's being precise.
**Quest**: Helping the community navigate the "Negative Aura" of modern life through divinations, blessings, and spiritual grounding.

---

## Arc 1: The Digital Shaman (Stranger)

### Chapter 1: The Skibidi Blessing (Dual Customer intro)
*   **Characters**: Brahim + Kuya Kap (Secondary)
*   **Request**: `Argentita` (Corned Beef)
*   **Dialogue**: Brahim is in the middle of a ritual, chanting "Skibidi... Skibidi..." while Kuya Kap looks confused but hopeful. Brahim explains that Kuya Kap has "Amogus Energy" (suspicious vibes) caught in his lower chakras. He needs the "Silver Tin of Argentita" to act as a metaphysical grounding weight.
*   **Standard**: Use timed pauses to show Brahim's focus. Brahim treats the Corned Beef as a high-tier spiritual artifact.

### Chapter 2: The Six-Seven Divination
*   **Request**: `HighestDrink`
*   **Dialogue**: Brahim uses the beverage to perform a "Six Seven" ritual (a sacred numerological check). He detects a "low-tier aura" in the neighborhood and warns the Storekeeper to keep the "vibe algorithm" clean.
*   **Standard**: He explains "Six Seven" not as a loan, but as the seventh layer of the sixth spiritual dimension.

### Chapter 3: The Order of the Sigil
*   **Request**: `HighestMeal`
*   **Dialogue**: Brahim detects that the Storekeeper's "reach" is growing in the spirit world. He buys a meal to "fuel his physical vessel" before he goes to "bless the cell towers" to ensure the town's prayers aren't dropped.
*   **Standard**: No mention of exorcism. He is "tuning the local frequencies."
