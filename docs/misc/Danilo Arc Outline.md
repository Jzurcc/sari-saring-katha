# Danilo — Story Arc Outline

### 3 Arcs · 9 Interactions · The Loiterer → The Omniscient → The Guardian

---

## Character Foundation

**Danilo** is the quintessential Filipino *tambay* (loiterer). He just hangs out by the sari-sari store counter, never actually buying anything, but always loaded with fresh *tsismis* (gossip) about every other character in the game. However, beneath his oversized sunglasses and fedora is his true identity: he is a **Daligmata**, a guardian creature from Visayan mythology covered entirely in eyes. He is literally built to see everything. His gossip isn't just nosiness; it is omniscience disguised as mundane chatter.

### Dialogue Filter (Global)

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Core Want** | To fulfill his sacred duty of watching over the town of Karimlan and ensuring everyone is safe, without breaking his mundane cover. |
| **Strategy** | Uses the universal language of neighborhood gossip. He frames his omniscient observations of people's private pains as "Just things I heard" or "Things I noticed." |
| **What He Hides** | The immense emotional burden of seeing everyone's tragedies simultaneously. Watching people hurt without directly interfering is exhausting. |
| **Voice** | Conspiratorial, casual, leans in close. Starts sentences with "Uy," "Psst," or "Between you and me." Friendly, but his insights are eerily, almost terrifyingly accurate. |

### The Arc at a Glance

```
ARC 1 — THE CHISMOSO         →  ARC 2 — THE MANY EYES        →  ARC 3 — THE WATCHER
He seems like a nosy uncle.     He knows impossible details.   He reveals his guardian nature.
Gossip is just fun banter.      Gossip turns into warnings.    He validates the player's work.
```

**Collectible Reward:** *Amber Spectacles* — A pair of heavily tinted, slightly oversized sunglasses. When you wear them, the edges of your vision feel sharper, as if you have eyes on the back of your head.

## ARC 1 — "THE CHISMOSO"

**Thematic Purpose:** Establish his role as the town's rumor mill. Use him to organically flesh out the lore of the other characters and the town. Provide relief from the transaction economy (a breather where no inventory management is needed).

**Arc-End State:** The player sees him as a harmless, slightly annoying but entertaining neighborhood fixture.

---

### Chapter 1 — *"The Free Real Estate"*

**Scene Beat:** Danilo introduces himself simply by taking up space. He leans on the counter, watches a customer leave (e.g., T.K. the Vloggger), and starts talking.

**Information to Communicate:**

- He is a fixture at the store.
- He does not buy anything. Ever.
- He knows about T.K.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To establish his territory at the store as his prime observation post.

**Subtext Through Commerce:**
None. He actively declines buying anything. His lack of commerce signals he is outside the material struggles of the other characters.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Uy, boss. Nice little setup you have here. Mind if I just lean here for a bit? Good shade."*
> *(He adjusts his oversized sunglasses).*
> *"I saw that vlogger walking away. Keeps talking to a camera. I swear I saw him walk past the same mango tree four times yesterday. Needs a better map, that one. Anyway, I won't buy anything, just hanging out."*

---

### Chapter 2 — *"The Cough"*

**Scene Beat:** Danilo drops some gossip about Kuya Kap.

**Information to Communicate:**

- He is observing Kap's health decline.
- He masks genuine concern as casual gossip.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To quietly warn the player to look out for Kap, using his "chismoso" persona.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Psst. Have you seen Kap lately? He's looking a bit gray."*
> *(He leans in, whispering like it's a huge secret).*
> *"I saw him sitting on his porch at 3 AM last night. Just holding a pack of cigarettes and coughing. He thinks nobody sees him when it's dark, but... I see a lot of things. You should check on him."*

---

### Chapter 3 — *"The Midnight Flight"*

**Scene Beat:** Danilo gossips about Manang Ana.

**Information to Communicate:**

- He knows the twins are Manananggals.
- He treats supernatural secrets as mundane neighborhood drama.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To vent about the chaos of the town while proving he misses absolutely nothing.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Is Ana okay? She bought a lot from you the other day."*
> *(He shakes his head, laughing slightly).*
> *"I don't blame her. I was on the roof last night and I saw two of her kids flying back from the next province. Wing coordination was terrible. Clipped a power line. Typical teenagers, right? Can't even fly straight."*

---

## ARC 2 — "THE MANY EYES"

**Thematic Purpose:** The player realizes Danilo's gossip isn't just good eyesight; it's omniscience. He sees things that happen in total privacy or isolation. The gossip turns from funny to chillingly accurate.

**Arc-End State:** The player suspects Danilo is not entirely human.

---

### Chapter 4 — *"The Private Conversation"*

**Scene Beat:** He recounts the private, emotionally devastating conversation between Buboy and Kuya Kap.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Danilo can see things he was physically nowhere near.
- The burden of his sight.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To process a painful thing he witnessed. He uses the player as a therapist.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Kids are brutal, aren't they?"*
> *(He rubs his forehead above his sunglasses as if dealing with a headache).*
> *"I was watching Buboy talk to Kap on Sunday. Kap was trying to hide the smoke, and the kid just asked him, innocent as day, why he didn't just throw the box away. I saw Kap's heart physically sink. Hard to watch... and I watch everything."*

---

### Chapter 5 — *"The Highway"*

**Scene Beat:** He gossips about Rosalyn. This is where his omniscience breaks the bounds of reality.

**Information to Communicate:**

- He knows Rosalyn is a spirit.
- He sees her trauma loop on the highway.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To express his sympathy for the trapped ghost, something mortals rarely do.

**Key Beat:**
> *"That pale girl who visits you... Rosalyn, right?"*
> *(He looks down at his hands, his voice dropping its gossipy tone).*
> *"She's a quiet one. I see her standing out on the old highway before she walks here. Every night. The cars just drive right through her, and she looks so confused every time. It’s a miracle she found her way to your store. You’re doing a good thing, letting her stay."*

---

### Chapter 6 — *"Too Bright"*

**Scene Beat:** Danilo complains about physical exhaustion. A momentary slip of his disguise.

**Information to Communicate:**

- He is physically covered in eyes.
- Watching over everyone is exhausting.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
A brief moment of complaining.

**Key Beat:**
> *(He touches his neck, his arms, his chest beneath his shirt).*
> *"Sakit sa ulo. Headache. Sometimes I wish I could just close my eyes and sleep. But when you have... as many as I do, there's always one that stays open."*
> *(He realizes what he said and quickly adjusts his sunglasses).*
> *"Uh, I mean... insomnia. Bad insomnia. Too much coffee, you know? Anyway, later boss."*

---

## ARC 3 — "THE WATCHER"

**Thematic Purpose:** The reveal. Danilo drops the gossip act entirely. He steps into his role as a Daligmata and openly acts as a guardian and narrator of the player's success.

**Arc-End State:** The town is safe, and the Watcher can finally rest.

---

### Chapter 7 — *"The Pale Ledger"*

**Scene Beat:** Danilo confronts the player about Reyna Mayari.

**Information to Communicate:**

- He knows about the overarching debt.
- He offers moral support.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To validate the player. He cannot interfere with the Queen, but he can encourage the mortal fighting her.

**Key Beat:**
> *"She’s been coming around. The Queen of Biringan."*
> *(He doesn't lean on the counter this time. He stands straight).*
> *"I watch her leave your store with her ledger. That's a heavy burden your grandmother left you. But I’ve been watching you, too. You aren’t cracking. You hold this town together better than she ever did."*

---

### Chapter 8 — *"The Daligmata"*

**Scene Beat:** He formally reveals what he is and thanks the player.

**Information to Communicate:**

- He is a Daligmata.
- The player has practically done his job for him.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To express genuine, unguarded gratitude.

**Key Beat:**
> *"You figured it out by now, right? Why I know so much?"*
> *(He lowers his sunglasses slightly. In the shadows, a dozen small, secondary irises blink briefly along his cheekbones).*
> *"I'm a Daligmata. My kind are built to be guardians. We watch over sickness, over lost things. Usually, mortals hate us because we see all their dirty secrets. But all I see in Karimlan are tired people trying their best. And I see you, giving them a place to rest."*

---

### Chapter 9 — *"The First Purchase"*

**Scene Beat:** The epilogue of his arc. The town is safe (the debt is paid, the arcs are resolved). Danilo does something he has never done before.

**Information to Communicate:**

- He is off-duty.
- He finally participates in the mortal world.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To enjoy life, not just observe it.

**Subtext Through Commerce:**
He buys a single `[HighestDrink]` or `[HighestSnack]`. His very first transaction.

**Key Beat:**
> *(He slams a few warm coins down on the counter).*
> *"Give me a `[HighestDrink]`, boss."*
> *(He grins as you hand it to him, popping the tab/seal).*
> *"Ah. First time I bought something here, huh? Now that everyone’s safe, I finally learned to blink for a few seconds. I don't need to watch so closely anymore."*
> *(He leaves a pair of sunglasses on the counter).*
> *"Keep those. See you around."*
