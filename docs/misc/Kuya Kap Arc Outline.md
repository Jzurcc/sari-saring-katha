# Kuya Kap — Story Arc Outline

### 3 Arcs · 9 Interactions · Cigarettes → Candy

---

## Character Foundation

**Kuya Kap** is a gentle giant — big frame, soft voice, disarming warmth. He started coming to the sari-sari store every day when your grandmother was still alive. He's been buying the same thing for years. To him, the store is a ritual. A safe harbor. He doesn't come here for the cigarettes. He comes here because it's the one place in his day where nobody expects anything from him.

### Dialogue Filter (Global)

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Core Want** | To feel like everything is fine. To not be the problem in someone else's day. |
| **Strategy** | Pre-emptive lightness. He makes himself easy — warm, self-deprecating, funny — so nobody looks too hard. |
| **What He Hides** | The cigarettes aren't a habit. They're load-bearing. Something heavy is sitting on top of this man and he doesn't have words for it. |
| **Voice** | Unhurried. Speaks mostly in English, seasoned lightly with Filipino words ("naman", "ha", "salamat", "hehe"). Compliments the store before saying what he needs. Talks about neighbors before talking about himself. Never starts a sentence with "I". |

### The Arc at a Glance

```
ARC 1 — THE REGULAR     →  ARC 2 — THE CRACK     →  ARC 3 — THE TURN
He is a fixture.            Something presses in.      He changes. It's real.
Player learns the shape.    Player sees behind it.     Player is part of why.
```

**Collectible Reward:** A smooth, pale white stone — *"Bato ng Maliwanag"* — which Kuya Kap has carried in his pocket for years. He says nothing about it. He just places it on the counter before he leaves for the last time.

---

## ARC 1 — "THE REGULAR"

**Thematic Purpose:** Establish the rhythm. Make the player feel like Kuya Kap is a feature of the world — reliable, warm, easy. So that when things shift, it *registers*. The player should love him before they understand him.

**Arc-End State:** The player knows Kuya Kap's name, face, routine, and a few details about his life. They have no reason to worry about him. That's the point.

---

### Chapter 1 — *"The New Face"*

**Day:** 1–2 (one of the first customers ever)
**Scene Beat:** First meeting. He arrives, notices the changing of the guard, and formally introduces himself. He acknowledges the player's circumstances and pays his respects to the grandmother's memory.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Kuya Kap is a regular and has been for a long time.
- He buys cigarettes. Specifically: [HighestCigarette], every time.
- He knew the grandmother and is pleasantly surprised to meet her grandchild.
- He's warm, easy to be around, and immediately likable.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He wants this transition to feel normal and welcoming. He's walking into a store that has a new face behind the counter, and instead of making it awkward, he takes it upon himself to make the *player* feel comfortable.

**What Else Is Happening:**
This is one of the player's first transactions. They're still learning the counter. Kuya Kap is patient — conspicuously patient.

**Subtext Through Commerce:**
He asks for the cigarettes by brand name without hesitation. He knows exactly where they used to be on the shelf. If the player moved them, he glances to the old spot first. He doesn't complain.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Oh, so you're the one taking over. Nice to finally meet you. I'm Kap, but everyone around here just calls me Kuya Kap. Your Lola always took good care of us... I hope I won't be too much trouble."*
> *(He smiles easily. You can tell he means it.)*

**Information Out:** Player learns his name, his brand, that he knew Lola, and that he is a polite, foundational member of the town.

---

### Chapter 2 — *"He Knows Everyone"*

**Day:** 3–5
**Scene Beat:** Kuya Kap arrives and immediately starts talking about *other people*. A neighbor's new roof. The tricycle driver who's always early. He knows everything about everyone in Karimlan.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Kuya Kap is deeply embedded in the community — a fixture, not a visitor.
- He observes everything but reveals nothing about himself.
- (Subtle) He deflects any question that points inward.
- World-building: refers to other neighbors and the town's rhythm.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He's genuinely interested in people. But right now, talking about other people is also easier than talking about himself. The deflection and the warmth are the same thing.

**What Else Is Happening:**
Another customer is waiting behind him. Kuya Kap notices and wraps up faster than he probably wants to. He's considerate even when it costs him.

**Subtext Through Commerce:**
Buys the cigarettes. This time, he glances at the candy jars briefly while paying. Just a look. Nothing said.

**Key Beat:**
> *"We've got the exact same schedule every day, huh? That lady over there, Manang Ana, she hasn't bought anything from you yet... she probably doesn't know you're fully open for business."*
> *(He uses inclusive language. He's already decided you're part of the neighborhood.)*

**Information Out:** Player understands the community. Gets a sense of who else is in Karimlan. Feels the warmth of Kuya Kap's social world — and doesn't notice yet that his own inner world is absent from the talk.

---

### Chapter 3 — *"Almost"*

**Day:** 6–8
**Scene Beat:** A small beat. Almost nothing. Kuya Kap arrives as usual, they exchange pleasantries. He reaches for the cigarettes — then his eyes land on the candy display. A pause. Then he orders the cigarettes anyway.

**Information to Communicate:**

- The first crack. Barely visible.
- He has noticed the candy before. This is not the first time, but it's the first time the player sees it.
- He's not ready. But something in him registered the option.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To leave without it being A Thing. He doesn't want the player to notice the pause. He recovers fast, smiles, pays, and goes. His whole body language says: *nothing to see here.*

**What Else Is Happening:**
The player might not even notice this scene if they're not paying attention — which is intentional. This is a "blink and you miss it" beat.

**Dialogue Option (Player):**
> *[Optional] "Kuya, do you want some candy too?"*
> — He laughs it off: *"Haha! Maybe next time na lang."*
> — If the player doesn't offer, just: *cigarettes, payment, warm goodbye.*

**Subtext Through Commerce:**
The [HighestCigarette] is purchased without hesitation in tone, but with a half-second of hesitation in his body. The candy goes unmentioned unless the player speaks first.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Hehe. Salamat, boss. See you tomorrow."*
> *(He always says "see you tomorrow." It's a promise to himself as much as to you.)*

**Information Out:** Arc 1 closes on a comfortable note. The "almost" is either noticed or not.

---

## ARC 2 — "THE CRACK"

**Thematic Purpose:** Something presses in. The player starts to understand that the lightness is a performance. There's weight underneath it, and it's getting harder to carry.

**Arc-End State:** Player knows about the neighbor kid Buboy who looks up to him. They know about a doctor's visit he doesn't take seriously. They feel the gap between who he presents and what's actually going on.

---

### Chapter 4 — *"Just a Bit Tired"*

**Day:** 10–12
**Scene Beat:** He shows up looking tired. Not dramatically tired — just the specific tiredness of a person who doesn't sleep well but has always managed. He jokes about it. The joke lands. But the player reads the body language.

**Information to Communicate:**

- His health is not great.
- He went to a doctor (mentioned in passing).
- He was told something and he doesn't want to dwell on it.
- He's still managing. Barely.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To make the store visit feel normal. He knows he looks rough. He gets ahead of it with a joke so the player doesn't ask. The joke is armor.

**What Else Is Happening:**
It's a busier day. He's self-effacing about taking up your time.

**Subtext Through Commerce:**
He almost buys a second pack of [HighestCigarette]. Changes his mind. Buys one. Pays exact change — he's already counted it in his pocket.

**Key Beat:**
> *"I went to the clinic yesterday. The doctor told me not to worry too much."*
> *(Beat. He smiles.)*
> *"He's just resting anyway. Doesn't really have any other job to do right now."*

**Information Out:** Player now knows about the clinic. The punchline is meant to close the topic — but it leaves an uneasy linger.

---

### Chapter 5 — *"For Buboy"*

**Day:** 13–16
**Scene Beat:** He brings up Buboy — the neighbor kid who always hangs around. Young. Looks up to Kuya Kap the way kids look up to the biggest person in the room.

**Information to Communicate:**

- Buboy exists and is important to Kuya Kap.
- Buboy made a comment that Kuya Kap is still turning over in his head.
- The cigarettes are now something seen through Buboy's eyes.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He wants to share a nice story about the kid. But the reason Buboy came up is sitting just below the surface of the anecdote — Kuya Kap is working through the guilt of being a role model with a bad habit.

**What Else Is Happening:**
This is a longer visit. He leans on the counter. The queue is light. Let the scene breathe.

**Dialogue Option (Player):**
> *"He's a good kid."*
> — Kuya Kap: *"Yeah, he is. Sometimes a little too smart for his own good, honestly."* *(Affectionate, but carries an underlying weight).*

**Subtext Through Commerce:**
He buys a [HighestCandy] candy. Separate from his usual purchase. He asks the player to pick one for "the kid."

**Key Beat:**
> *"That kid from down the street, Buboy... he asked me on Sunday, 'Kuya Kap, why don't you just throw that away?' Kids, right? They don't know it's not that easy."*
> *(He laughs gently. Then he's quiet for a moment. Then he pays.)*

**Information Out:** Buboy asked the hard question. Kuya Kap is carrying that question with him.

---

### Chapter 6 — *"A Short Pause"*

**Day:** 17–20
**Scene Beat:** He skips a day. When he comes back, he's quieter. He doesn't explain the absence unless asked. He still buys the cigarettes. But something is visibly *off*.

**Information to Communicate:**

- He's been thinking.
- He had a private moment that shifted something.
- He's not performing his old, easy self.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He doesn't know exactly what he wants. He came out of routine, but seems a little lost. He won't fully hide it, but won't explain it either.

**What Else Is Happening:**
Keep this scene quiet. Minimal background activity. His stillness is the star of the scene.

**Dialogue Option (Player):**
> *[Optional] "Is everything okay, Kuya?"*
> — If yes: *"Of course. It was just a bit too windy earlier."* *(He deflects but stays an extra beat longer.)*
> — If player says nothing: he buys, pays, pauses at the door, then leaves.

**Subtext Through Commerce:**
He buys the cigarettes, puts them in his pocket. He holds his lighter but doesn't strike it. He puts it away and walks off.

**Key Beat:**
> *(If asked how he is)*
> *"I'm doing alright. Alright, salamat. See you tomorrow."*
> — But this time, "see you tomorrow" sounds like he's trying to convince himself.

**Information Out:** Arc 2 ends in suspension. Something is turning, but it hasn't resolved.

---

## ARC 3 — "THE TURN"

**Thematic Purpose:** The payoff. The cigarettes-to-candy transition. It’s quiet and personal, witnessed only by the player. The player didn't fix him; they provided the space he needed to fix himself.

**Arc-End State:** Kuya Kap buys candy for himself. He gives the player the old stone.

---

### Chapter 7 — *"Taking His Time"*

**Day:** 21–24
**Scene Beat:** He arrives and spends far longer than usual at the counter. He talks about Buboy. About the neighborhood. About the grandmother — the first time he's brought her up deeply. He's stalling.

**Information to Communicate:**

- He's made a decision but is hesitant to enact it.
- The grandmother had a part to play in his relationship with the store.
- He's saying goodbye to the ritual.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He wants to finally speak up. He circles the topic with stories. He needs a patient listener.

**What Else Is Happening:**
A quiet day. The silence between sentences does the heavy lifting.

**Subtext Through Commerce:**
He doesn't buy the cigarettes until the very end, fishing for them reluctantly.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Your Lola... she actually hid a stone in the candy jar once. I never really understood why. But... hm."*
> *(He leaves the thought unfinished, and departs.)*

**Information Out:** The player now knows about the stone in the candy jar. Lola saw something in him. Kuya Kap is on the precipice.

---

### Chapter 8 — *"Not Today"*

**Day:** 25–28
**Scene Beat:** He arrives, asks for the usual. Then, after a heavy pause, he takes it back: *"Actually... not today."* He stands there, testing the new reality.

**Information to Communicate:**

- He is quitting.
- He shrinks away from making a speech about it.
- He's afraid of the vulnerability, and hopes the player doesn't make a big deal of it.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He wants to change course without it being an 'event'. The best thing the player can do is respond *normally*.

**What Else Is Happening:**
The store is busy enough to give him some cover during his pause. The transaction tray remains empty.

**Dialogue Option (Player) — this one matters:**
> *"Alright, Kuya."*
> — The correct response. No fanfare. Just quiet support.

**Subtext Through Commerce:**
He pays for nothing, feels momentarily awkward, then buys a bottle of [HighestDrink] instead. He holds it like an anchor.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Hm. Even that stone of mine... Buboy told me I should give it to the right person."*
> *(He pats his pocket.)*
> *"It's like the kid knows something."*

**Information Out:** The stone is in play. The player knows what is coming next.

---

### Chapter 9 — *"Candy"*

**Day:** 29–32
**Scene Beat:** He arrives. Same time, same warmth. He doesn't mention cigarettes. He looks straight at the candy display.

> *"Just one of those [HighestCandy] candies today."*

He pays, unwraps it, and eats it at the counter. He looks out over the town, filled with a quiet peace. He reaches into his pocket.

**Information to Communicate:**

- He did it.
- The stone was Lola's, then his, and now belongs to the player.
- He will be back tomorrow.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
He wants this to feel like a normal day. And it is—except for the candy and the gift.

**What Else Is Happening:**
Nothing. The earned void scene. Strip everything away. Just Kuya Kap, the player, and the sweet wrapper.

**Subtext Through Commerce:**
He doesn't ask for the price. He just hands over a bill, takes his change, and doesn't count it.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Your Lola... she was a good woman. She told me once, 'Kap, give that to whoever owns the store next.'"*
> *(He places the smooth, pale white stone on the counter.)*
> *"I didn't think the right time would come. But here we are. Hehe."*
> *(He pauses, chewing thoughtfully.)*
> *"This [HighestCandy] is actually pretty good."*

**Collectible Unlocked:** 🪨 *Bato ng Maliwanag* — *"A smooth, pale stone. Kuya Kap says it keeps the house warm."*
