# Sarimanok — Story Arc Outline

### 3 Arcs · 9 Interactions · The Nuisance → The Auditor → The 1980s Gentleman

---

## Character Foundation

**The Sarimanok** is a legendary, vibrantly colorful bird from Philippine mythology, traditionally depicted holding a fish in its beak. In *Sari-Saring Katha*, he is a total gag character. For the vast majority of the game, the player will assume he is just an annoying, overly aggressive bird that wanders into the store, screams, breaks things, and occasionally drops coins in exchange for snacks. However, in a complete subversion of expectations, Arc 3 reveals he is actually incredibly articulate, highly educated, and speaks exactly like a high-society gentleman from the 1980s.

### Dialogue Filter (Global)

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Core Want** | To conduct his business transactions efficiently, though for the first two arcs he believes humans are too stupid to understand him, so he just screams at them. |
| **Strategy** | Aggressive, judgmental avian staring, followed by absolute refined elegance. |
| **What He Hides** | The fact that he could speak perfect English the entire time. |
| **Voice** | First two arcs: "Ba-kawk!" accompanied by intense physical actions. Arc three: "Ba-kawk!" followed by impeccable, vocabulary-rich 1980s gentlemanly prose (e.g., "I say," "Splendid," "My good man"). |

### The Scene Architecture (Masterclass Applied)

* **The Timer:** As a bird, he is violently impatient. He taps his talons on the floor loudly. If the player takes too long, he pecks the counter.
* **The Collision:** He causes physical chaos. He knocks cans over, stares unblinkingly at the player's ledger, and steals shiny things.
* **Subtext Through Commerce:** During his "bird" phase, he doesn't hand over money; he spits out damp, dirty coins from his beak. During his "gentleman" phase, he slides neatly folded bills across the counter with his wing.

### The Arc at a Glance

```
ARC 1 — THE NUISANCE         →  ARC 2 — THE ESCALATION       →  ARC 3 — THE GENTLEMAN
He just screams at you.         He actively judges you.        He reveals his true self.
No transactions at first.       He steals snacks.              He requests fine goods politely.
```

**Collectible Reward:** *Golden Calling Card* — A shiny golden feather that, upon closer inspection, has an embossed 1980s corporate watermark on it.

## ARC 1 — "THE NUISANCE"

**Thematic Purpose:** Establish him as the game's gag character. The player should view him as nothing more than a weird, annoying animal encounter.

**Arc-End State:** The player expects him to just walk in and scream.

---

### Chapter 1 — *"The Stare"*

**Scene Beat:** First meeting. He appears at the counter. He does not buy anything.

**Information to Communicate:**

* He is a bird.
* He is very loud.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To assert dominance over this new retail establishment.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Ba-kawk!"*
> *"Ba-kawk!"*
> *"Ba-kawk!"*

---

### Chapter 2 — *"The Judgment"*

**Scene Beat:** He returns. He still refuses to act like a normal customer.

**Information to Communicate:**

* He seems to be judging the player.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To inspect the inventory.

**What Else Is Happening:**
He tilts his head upside down to look at the player.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Ba-kawk!"*
> *"Ba-kawk!"*
> *"Ba-kawk!"*

---

### Chapter 3 — *"The Theft"*

**Scene Beat:** He finally takes an item, establishing that he is at least part of the store's economy.

**Information to Communicate:**

* He pays, but barely.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
A snack, but he refuses to ask nicely.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Ba-kawk!"*
> *"Ba-kawk!"*
> *"Ba-kawk! Ba-kawk!"*

---

## ARC 2 — "THE ESCALATION"

**Thematic Purpose:** Build the comedy. The player assumes he represents pure chaos, but his actions hint that he's actually highly calculating.

**Arc-End State:** The player sees him as an aggressive auditor who just happens to be a bird.

---

### Chapter 4 — *"The Mess"*

**Scene Beat:** He causes a mess entirely on purpose to get what he wants.

**Information to Communicate:**

* He is impatient.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
A beverage. Immediately.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Ba-kawk!"* *(He begins aggressively pecking the counter to get your attention).*
> *"Ba-kawk!"* *(He points his wing toward the refrigerator, demanding a `[HighestDrink]`. When you hand it to him, he drops coins from his beak and chugs the bottle).*

---

### Chapter 5 — *"The Heavy Sigh"*

**Scene Beat:** He acts exhausted by the player's lack of efficiency.

**Information to Communicate:**

* He thinks he is better than you.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To run his errand without having to deal with "human incompetence."

**Key Beat:**
> *"Ba-kawk!"* *(He waits in line behind another customer. He taps his talons rhythmically: tap-tap-tap-tap).*
> *"Ba-kawk! ... Ba-kawk."* *(The second "Ba-kawk" sounds suspiciously like a heavy, exasperated sigh. He pushes a very old, tarnished coin across the counter, expecting a `[HighestCannedGood]` in return).*

---

### Chapter 6 — *"The Audit"*

**Scene Beat:** The climax of the "Bird" phase. He closely inspects the store's finances.

**Information to Communicate:**

* He is analyzing the shop's success.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To review your Q3 earnings.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Ba-kawk! Ba-kawk! Ba-kawk!"* *(He leans far over the counter, completely ignoring the `[HighestCandy]` you offer him. Instead, he flips open your ledger with his beak. He stares at the numbers. He nods approvingly).*
> *"Ba-kawk."* *(He takes the candy, leaves a crisp five-peso coin, and walks away with his chest puffed out).*

---

## ARC 3 — "THE GENTLEMAN"

**Thematic Purpose:** The punchline. The pure absurdity of an eloquently speaking legendary bird.

**Arc-End State:** The player realizes they were being judged by a 1980s corporate mogul the entire time.

---

### Chapter 7 — *"The Clearing of the Throat"*

**Scene Beat:** The great reveal. He finally speaks.

**Information to Communicate:**

* He can talk.
* He sounds like a wealthy 1980s socialite.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To apologize for his barbaric behavior now that he has decided the player is worthy of his conversational time.

**What Else Is Happening:**
He adjusts imaginary cuffs on his wings.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Ba-kawk! ...ahem."* *(He clears his throat. When he speaks, his voice is deep, smooth, and impeccably posh).*
> *"Ba-kawk! My most profound apologies, proprietor. I had something dreadfully stuck in my throat for the past few weeks. It is utterly uncivilized to communicate via screeching."*
> *"Ba-kawk! I must say, your inventory management has improved substantially. Might I procure your finest `[HighestDrink]`, please?"*

---

### Chapter 8 — *"The Macroeconomics"*

**Scene Beat:** He drops casual, high-society conversation as if the first six interactions never happened.

**Information to Communicate:**

* He is a businessman.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To discuss the local economy over a fine good.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Ba-kawk! Splendid day to you, my good man/woman. Simply resplendent."* *(He politely waits for you to fetch a `[HighestCoffee]`).*
> *"Ba-kawk! Tell me, what are your thoughts on the macroeconomic climate of Karimlan? The fluctuating interest rates of the Pale Queen's quotas must put a ghastly strain on your overhead. Very resilient of you to weather the storm. Ba-kawk!"*

---

### Chapter 9 — *"The Calling Card"*

**Scene Beat:** The epilogue. He bids the player farewell to attend to his portfolio.

**Information to Communicate:**

* He is leaving.
* He leaves his collectible.

**Character Filter — What He Wants:**
To formally conclude his business here.

**Key Beat:**
> *"Ba-kawk! Alas, I must take my leave of your charming establishment today."* *(He gives a surprisingly graceful, aristocratic bow).*
> *"Ba-kawk! The stock market awaits no avian, as they say. Do keep up the marvelous work. I have left my card, should you ever wish to discuss a franchise opportunity."* *(He walks away from the counter, leaving behind a dazzling, golden feather).*

**Collectible Unlocked:** 🪶 *Golden Calling Card* — *"A brilliantly colored, golden feather. Strangely, it has a watermark embossed into the vane, and it smells faintly of very expensive 1980s cologne."*
